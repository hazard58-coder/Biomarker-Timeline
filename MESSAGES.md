# Five first-contact messages

Fill-in-the-blank templates for reaching people you already know who are on
TRT/HRT/peptides. Each is under 80 words. Each: says you built a tool, offers
**one free or half-price trial report** in exchange for honest feedback, asks
for their lab PDFs. No hype — keep it the way you'd actually talk.

Replace the bracketed slots before sending. Pick the variant that matches the
relationship; don't send the same copy to everyone.

---

## 1 — Existing wellness-coaching contact already on TRT

> Hey [NAME] — I built a small tool I want your eyes on. You send me your lab
> PDFs from the last few draws and it turns them into one report that charts
> every marker over time, with each lab's own range shaded in. It just organizes
> your numbers — no advice. Want a free copy of yours in exchange for honest
> feedback? If so, send me the PDFs.

*(~70 words)*

---

## 2 — Friend who's mentioned starting testosterone

> Hey [NAME] — you mentioned you started test. I made a thing that takes your lab
> PDFs and charts each marker across your draws so you can actually see the
> trend, next to the lab's own ranges. Early days, so I'm giving the first ones
> free for honest feedback. Want yours? Just send me whatever lab PDFs you've got
> so far.

*(~62 words)*

---

## 3 — Someone from the gym / optimization world

> [NAME] — quick one. I built a tool that pulls every biomarker out of your lab
> PDFs and trends it over time, flagging anything outside the lab's printed
> range. It's data organization, not advice — something to bring to your doctor.
> I'll do yours free if you'll tell me honestly whether it's useful. Send the
> PDFs and I'll turn them around.

*(~63 words)*

---

## 4 — Person in an online TRT/peptide community you're part of

> Hey [NAME] — I've been lurking in [COMMUNITY] long enough to know we all have
> labs buried in five different portals. I built something that takes your lab
> PDFs and charts each marker over time against the lab's own ranges. Giving a
> few away free for honest feedback before I price it. Want one? DM me your PDFs
> and the name to put on it.

*(~71 words)*

---

## 5 — Past client you've lost touch with

> Hey [NAME] — been a while, hope you're well. I built a tool I think you'd
> actually use: send your lab PDFs and it makes one report charting every marker
> across all your draws, with each lab's range shown. No interpretation, just
> your data laid out to bring to your doctor. I'd love to do yours half-price as
> a thank-you. Want me to?

*(~70 words)*

---

### Notes for sending

- Lead with the relationship, not the product. The first line should sound like you.
- One ask per message: their lab PDFs. Don't stack requests.
- If they say yes, point them at the price after they've seen the value:
  **$79 one-time**, or **$29/month** to keep it updated each draw.
- Never imply the report tells them what their results mean. It organizes; their
  physician interprets.
