What it is

A single PDF report. It reads the lab PDFs you already have — from any lab, across as many draw dates as you’ve collected — pulls out every biomarker, and trends each one over time next to that lab’s own printed reference range. It organizes and visualizes your data. It does not interpret it.

Who it’s for

  • People on TRT, HRT, or peptide protocols who draw bloodwork every 8–12 weeks.
  • Anyone tracking a panel over time — testosterone, estradiol, SHBG, hematocrit, PSA, LH, lipids, and more.
  • People who want one document to bring to their physician instead of a stack of portal logins.

What you get

  • A trend chart for every biomarker — value over time, with the lab’s own reference range shaded in.

FAQ

Is this medical advice?
No. Biomarker Timeline is a data-organization report. It transcribes and charts the values from the lab PDFs you provide, next to each lab’s own printed reference range. It does not interpret those values, diagnose any condition, or recommend any treatment. Review all results with a licensed physician.
Which labs does it work with?
Any lab that gives you a PDF — LabCorp, Quest, hospital and clinic portals, and others. You can combine multiple labs and as many draw dates as you have.
How do I get my lab PDFs?
Download them from your lab’s patient portal (LabCorp, Quest, MyChart, etc.) — usually under Results or Documents, with a “Download PDF” option. Upload those files as-is.
What biomarkers does it track?
Whatever is printed on your labs — for example total and free testosterone, estradiol, SHBG, LH, hematocrit, PSA, and a lipid panel. Each marker that appears across your draws gets its own trend.
What happens to my files?
They’re used only to build your report, then deleted. Your files are not stored or shared.
What’s the difference between $79 and $29/month?
$79 is a single report from the PDFs you upload. $29/month keeps your timeline updated with each new draw — upload a new PDF and regenerate whenever you get fresh labs.
How do I cancel the subscription?
Sign in and open the billing portal to cancel in a click, or email contact@vitalisforge.com.
Can I bring it to my doctor?
Yes — that’s the point. The report is built to be printed and handed to your physician at your next appointment.
